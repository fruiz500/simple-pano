2 · Retouch (optional)

Do this after the photos are aligned.

A. In the main view

Select a photo and paint over a ghost or person in it with the left button: the other overlapping photos show through at once.

3°

Select a photo: it is shown solid so you can see what it contributes. Paint over the ghost or person in it; the other overlapping photos then show through. Select the next photo to brush it too. [ and ] change the brush size. Erase mode, or holding Alt, gives the paint back; with Erase mode on, Alt paints. Right-drag turns the view, the wheel zooms. Esc ends brushing.

B. In a separate window

Opens a magnified view. Paint over something or a gap and fill it with a local fill or an AI editor, or brush a ghost out of a photo.

C. The background panorama (after a bake)

Retouch the loaded or just baked background panorama itself, to remove artifacts found after the bake. The edit changes the panorama, not the photos, so save it afterwards. The View and Width boxes above apply here too; a smaller width gives more detail.

Results of the retouches (keep or take out each one, delete) are listed in the retouch window.

D. A logo at the nadir

Covers the tripod and your shadow with a logo or a round picture, as if it lay on the floor under the camera. It goes into the background panorama if there is one, otherwise over the stitched photos (and is included when you bake). The top of the logo points toward the middle of the panorama image.
